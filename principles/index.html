Principles

A record and an instrument. These are the rules it keeps.

Markets are loud. Cycles are quiet and old. CardCycles watches the quiet part, and writes it down every week.

  1. 01

    Every card has a cycle.

    Interest rises and fades. Supply follows. The pattern is older than any card.

  2. 02

    Read the long series before the latest point.

    One number is a moment. The series is the story.

  3. 03

    Seal the observation before the outcome is known.

    We write it down first, so the record can never be fitted to what happened.

  4. 04

    State the limits of every measure.

    Each series says plainly what it cannot tell you.

  5. 05

    Append. Never revise.

    Corrections are new entries. The past stays exactly as it was written.
